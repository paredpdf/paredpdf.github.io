Pared

Fix the page size, crop the margins, shrink the file. 100% in your browser, nothing uploaded.

Drop a PDF or photos here

Pared finds the real page inside a sheet that was exported at the wrong size (a portrait slide on a landscape page, for example), crops to it, and can shrink the file.

Choose several to merge them, or to fix them all the same way. Photos of a document become its pages.

How it works

Private

Your PDF never leaves this device. Pared runs entirely in your browser, and keeps working offline once it has loaded.

Finds the real page

It reads the outline the PDF marks for the page, or looks at where the content sits, then crops every page to it.

And more

Keep, reorder and rotate pages, shrink photos to a sensible resolution, turn photos into a PDF, add page numbers or a watermark, fill in forms, and sign.
